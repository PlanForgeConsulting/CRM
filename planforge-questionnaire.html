Controlled Group — Family Employees
Who qualifies as a controlled group family member?
Under IRC attribution rules, the following relatives of an owner are considered related parties: spouse, children (and grandchildren), parents (and grandparents), and siblings. If any of these family members work at the company, they are classified differently for plan testing purposes.
